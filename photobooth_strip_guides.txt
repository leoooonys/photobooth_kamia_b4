Notes for layout editing:
  - Standard portrait layouts are built from a grid.
  - Landscape layouts use custom geometry.
  - In landscape layouts, the big photo height stays fixed relative to the layout math, and the design/footer area is shorter in length.
  - The left landscape layout places the 3 small photos at the bottom row.
  - The right landscape layout places the 3 small photos at the top row.
